GeoKnight GIS · public demo · Duval County, Florida

GeoRescue scans public property maps for drawing mistakes and shows each one on a map, in plain English.

County parcel maps are the outlines of every property lot. Sometimes those outlines overlap, leave holes, or don't match the deed. GeoRescue checks every parcel in the county's public map automatically, flags what looks wrong, and explains why. Nothing is changed in any official record.

How it works, in 3 steps

  1. 1Load the public mapGeoRescue downloads Duval County's public parcel map (City of Jacksonville / Property Appraiser) on an automatic schedule.
  2. 2Check every parcelAutomated tests look for overlaps, lots inside other lots, holes, slivers, broken shapes and missing information.
  3. 3Explain and proposeEach problem is flagged with a plain explanation. For a few cases, the recorded deed is read and compared. Fixes are suggestions for a person to review, never applied to official data.

Reading the map

Each colour is one kind of problem. Zoomed out you see a heat map of where problems cluster; zoom in and click a parcel for details.

  • Contained parcel one lot drawn on top of a bigger one instead of cut out of it
  • Overlap two parcels claim the same piece of ground
  • Gap a small hole between parcels that no parcel covers
  • Sliver a tiny or very thin strip, often a leftover of lines that don't meet
  • Invalid shape an outline that breaks basic drawing rules, e.g. crosses itself
  • Missing information a required field, such as owner name, is blank

Questions

Does this change the county's records?

No. GeoRescue works on its own copy. The only automatic fix is repairing broken shapes in that copy; everything else is a proposal for review.

Is a flag proof something is wrong with my property?

No. A flag means the public map looks inconsistent. Many flags are harmless drawing quirks. Only a licensed surveyor, an attorney and the official records can settle a boundary.

What is the "featured test case" below?

One example where a smaller lot appears to be drawn inside a larger parcel. GeoRescue read the larger parcel's deed and compared its described shape to the map. Owner names are replaced with "Parcel A" and "Parcel B".

How often is it updated?

The public demo is set to re-run automatically once a week. The date of the latest run is shown under "Latest run".

How the test analysis works Proposed · test result

  1. FlaggedThe Parcel B lot appears to sit fully inside the Parcel A polygon — a contained_parcel flag, not an edge sliver.
  2. Appraiser mismatchThe Parcel A legal description states 7.83 ac; GIS shows 10.59 ac. The Parcel B lot is Lot 3 of a 2003 plat.
  3. Deed pulledOfficial Records 19870-841 (2021 warranty deed), OCR'd and parsed call by call (OCR may contain errors).
  4. Traverse closes 1:3533636 calls, misclosure 0.007 ft. A best-fit placement matches GIS minus the Parcel B lot at IoU 0.981, which suggests that lot lies outside the deed.
  5. Verdict proposed for reviewThe test result suggests cutting the Parcel B lot from the Parcel A polygon — a proposal, not a finding, and not applied. Open the case file →
